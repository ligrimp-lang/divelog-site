Dive log

Shearwater dive import for iPhone

Download on the App Store

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Your Shearwater dive computer records the whole dive. Dive log draws all of it.

For Shearwater computers only. Turn on Bluetooth on the computer and open the app: new dives come in. No cable, no account, no typing.

Each dive

  • The full profile: depth, temperature, stops and gas switches
  • Stops done against stops required, on a decompression dive
  • CNS and ascent rate
  • Every gas with its pressures and SCR (SAC), surface consumption rate

Your log

  • Dives named by place and time: "Canyon, Monday morning dive"
  • 9,000 dive sites, reefs and wrecks, found as you type, nearest first
  • Buddies, tags, notes and rating
  • Site, cylinders or buddies changed on many dives at once

Share

A card of your dive, made for posting on social media: a picture or a six-second video, story-sized.

Tested on the Perdix 2. A log, not a dive computer: your computer and your training own the dive.
